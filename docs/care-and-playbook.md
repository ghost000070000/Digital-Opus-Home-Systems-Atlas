# Care and playbooks

## Care

Each item can record a next-service date and a warranty date. Care lists:

- Overdue service
- Service and warranties in the next 90 / 60 days
- Recorded spend rolled up by room and system

Totals only include prices you typed. They are not an appraisal.

## Playbook

Critical items, current failures, and computed single points of failure get a card:

- Your written steps
- Modeled hard-affected items
- Recorded alternatives (backs-up, spareFor, sibling endpoints)

Contacts on that page are household names stored locally. Print cards for a paper copy.

This is still a dependency model, not emergency-services advice.
